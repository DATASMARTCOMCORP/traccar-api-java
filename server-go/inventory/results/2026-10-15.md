## Schema (`databasechangelog`, 58 rows; ids in `internal/store/testdata/production-changelog.tsv`)

- **Legacy history:** 25 `MARK_RAN` rows from Traccar 3.x and the old 4.0 path, from the original install.
- **Main history:** everything from 4.0-clean through 6.13.0, applied with Liquibase 4.23.2–5.0.1. 6.11.0, 6.13.0 and `changelog-6.13-dsc` were applied on 2026-06-04.
- **SIR DSC changeset present:** `tc_geofences.area` is `text`. It's needed: 212 geofences exceed 4096 characters, and the longest is 269,936.
- **Missing:** `changelog-6.13.0-fk-linkeddeviceid` and `changelog-6.15.0`. Ids such as `tc_devices.positionid` are still `integer`.
- **Lock:** the Liquibase lock is free. No TimescaleDB.

The Go schema check accepts exactly this history (`TestProductionChangelogAccepted`). If someone
deploys the repository's `schema/`, Java will apply the two missing changesets on its next start.

## Protocols

Suntech, Intellitrac and Atrack have no traffic in production.
